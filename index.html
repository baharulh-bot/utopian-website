    Frequently Asked Questions

    Assam and North-Eastern India are designated as Seismic Zone V by the Bureau of Indian Standards (IS 1893:2016)—the highest earthquake risk zone. Buildings without proper ductile detailing, response spectrum analysis, and shear wall integration face high risks of catastrophic structural collapse during earthquakes. Utopian's IIT and NIT-trained structural engineers calculate exact seismic load combinations to ensure zero structural failure.
    NDT testing evaluates the strength and integrity of existing concrete without breaking or damaging the structure. We use digital Schmidt Rebound Hammers, Ultrasonic Pulse Velocity (UPV), and rebar cover meters. You should commission an NDT audit if you are purchasing an existing commercial property, planning to add extra floors, noticing cracks or corrosion, or retrofitting older school/hospital buildings.
    Without a professional Bill of Quantities (BOQ) and Bar Bending Schedule (BBS), construction projects regularly suffer 20% to 35% material wastage and contractor inflation. Utopian prepares itemized CPWD/APWD schedule rates, exact steel cut lists, and milestone verification so you only pay for genuine quantities executed on site.
